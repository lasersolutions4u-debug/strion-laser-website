How Laser Cleaning Works

A clean, precise, and environmentally friendly alternative to traditional cleaning methods.

High-Energy Pulses

Short, intense laser pulses strike the surface, creating rapid thermal expansion that breaks the bond between contaminants and substrate.

Substrate Safe

The base material remains undamaged. Laser parameters are tuned so only the contaminant layer absorbs enough energy to be removed.

Zero Consumables

No chemicals, no abrasives, no blasting media. Only electricity required. No secondary waste to dispose of or hazardous materials to handle.

Applications

Rust & Corrosion Removal

Remove rust and oxidation from steel components, structures, and equipment. Clean without damaging the underlying metal surface.

Paint & Coating Removal

Strip paint, powder coating, and surface treatments from metal parts. Selective removal without damaging the substrate.

Mold Cleaning

Clean injection molds, die-cast molds, and tooling without disassembly. Remove release agents, residues, and buildup in-place.

Oil & Grease Removal

Degrease metal surfaces prior to welding, painting, or bonding. Eliminates solvent use and drying time.

Weld Preparation & Cleanup

Clean weld seams before welding for improved quality. Remove post-weld discoloration and oxidation for a clean finish.

Heritage Restoration

Gentle cleaning of historical metal artifacts, statues, and architectural elements. Preserves patina while removing harmful deposits.
