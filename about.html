About STRION LASER

Sichuan STRION Laser Technology Co., Ltd. is a national high-tech enterprise founded in 2017, integrating R&D, manufacturing, sales, and service of advanced laser equipment. Headquartered in Chengdu, Sichuan, STRION LASER serves customers in over 80 countries worldwide.

The company specializes in fiber laser technology with a focus on handheld laser welding and laser cleaning applications. STRION LASER is one of the pioneers in applying refrigerant direct cooling technology to handheld laser welders, solving the fundamental trade-off between compact size and continuous high-power operation.

STRION LASER is a cooperative partner brand represented by Jinan Euchio Machinery Co., Ltd., providing global customers with sales, technical support, and service for STRION LASER products.
