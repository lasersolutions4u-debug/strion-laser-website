Core Technology

Five proprietary technologies that set STRION LASER apart from conventional water-cooled and air-cooled systems.

976nm Pump Source

85% electro-optical conversion efficiency vs. ~70% for standard 915nm pumps. Higher efficiency means less power consumption and slower power degradation over the machine's lifetime.

14μm Small-Core Fiber

Industry-leading small core diameter delivers superior beam quality and energy density. Achieves ~40% deeper penetration than conventional 20μm fibers at the same power level.

Dual-Circuit Refrigerant Cooling

Compressor-driven refrigerant direct cooling with dual independent circuits. Enables true 7×24 continuous operation from -30°C to 60°C. The only system in its class with this capability.

All-in-One Integration

Laser source, cooling, control, and welding gun integrated into a single compact unit. Weighs 38-70kg (model dependent) — significantly lighter than water-cooled systems (80kg+).

Fully In-House Manufactured

Laser source, cooling unit, and welding gun are all designed and manufactured in-house. Complete control over quality, compatibility, and continuous improvement.

8-Layer Safety Protection

Comprehensive safety system protects both operator and equipment. Small-core fiber naturally reduces return light for safe welding of reflective materials like aluminum and copper.
