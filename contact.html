Frequently Asked Questions

STR-HW uses dual-circuit refrigerant direct cooling, which provides far superior cooling capacity compared to air-cooled systems. This enables higher power output (up to 3000W), true 24/7 continuous operation, reliable performance in extreme temperatures (-30°C to 60°C), deeper weld penetration via 14μm small-core fiber, and significantly slower power degradation over the machine's lifetime. Air-cooled systems are limited to under 1000W and cannot sustain continuous operation.

STR-HW welds stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum, galvanized sheet, brass, and copper. The 14μm small-core fiber provides inherent anti-reflection protection, making it particularly safe for highly reflective materials like aluminum and copper alloys. Welding thickness ranges from 0.5mm to 10mm depending on the model and material.
